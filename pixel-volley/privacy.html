Pixel Volley

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Pixel Volley ("the game") is made by Yunus Burkut, an independent developer in Istanbul, Türkiye ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the game and the services it uses collect, why, and the choices you have.

What I collect myself

Nothing that identifies you. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. Your level, coins, boosters, lives and sound and haptics settings are stored only on your device.

Services in the game

The game uses the following third-party services. Each one collects data under its own privacy policy.

  • AppLovin MAX (advertising and ad mediation), together with the ad networks it works with. It may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, coarse location derived from it, device model and OS version, and how you interact with ads. Used to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, if you allow it, personalise them. AppLovin privacy policy.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config). Collects gameplay events (for example which level started and whether it was won), app instance identifiers, device and OS information and crash reports. Used to understand how the game is played, fix crashes and tune difficulty. Analytics starts only after your consent choice. Firebase privacy information.
  • RevenueCat (in-app purchases). Receives your App Store purchase history and an anonymous app user id, to unlock and restore what you bought. RevenueCat privacy policy.
  • Apple handles all payments. I never see your payment details.

Tracking and consent

On first launch the game asks for your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EU, the UK and some US states) and shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you do not allow tracking, the game does not access your IDFA and you will see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings (Privacy Settings) or in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Buying Remove Ads or the Super Pack turns off interstitial ads. Rewarded videos you choose to watch still come through AppLovin MAX.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided data through the game, contact me and I will help have it removed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK or CCPA) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. Because I hold no data that identifies you, most requests are best sent to the services above; I am glad to help with any of them. Deleting the game removes everything stored on your device.

Retention and security

Each service keeps data for the period set out in its own policy. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
