Support

Found a bug, lost a purchase or have an idea? Write to yunus.burkut@outlook.com with your device model and iOS version. I usually answer within two working days.

Questions

I bought Remove Ads or the Super Pack on another device. How do I get it back?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Do coin packs come back with Restore Purchases?

No. Coins are used up as you play and are kept only on the device you bought them on, so deleting the game loses them. If a coin pack you paid for never arrived, email me the date and I will sort it out.

How do I change my ad and tracking choices?

Settings > Privacy Settings in the game, or iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

I saw an inappropriate ad.

Tap Report an Ad in the game's Settings, or email me what you saw and roughly when.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
